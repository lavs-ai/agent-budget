## Limitations

- Pricing can change after the review date.
- Cached tokens, cache writes, tool calls and long-context surcharges are not yet modelled.
- Copilot dollar output represents estimated AI-credit value, not necessarily an additional invoice charge.
- Included allowances and employer-specific agreements are not modelled.
- Token counts and output-to-input ratios are estimated before execution.
